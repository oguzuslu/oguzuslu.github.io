Ten Plane — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Ten Plane (“the app”) is a single-player arcade game published by Oğuz Uslu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what your choices are.

Data we collect ourselves

None. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. High scores and gameplay statistics are saved locally on your device only and never leave it.

Data collected by third-party services

The app displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To deliver ads, AdMob may collect a device identifier (such as the vendor identifier) and information related to the ads shown.

Ten Plane requests Google’s limited ads for every user, in every region, together with the non-personalized ads flag (npa=1). In this mode ads are not tailored to you, and Google does not use a device identifier for personalization, frequency capping, or ad measurement. Your IP address is still used to deliver the ad to your device, and Google may use limited local storage solely to detect invalid traffic and fraud.

The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, does not display Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, and does not access your Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

Google’s handling of the data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is described in the Google Privacy & Terms.

Diagnostics

If you opt in to sharing diagnostic data through your iOS device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash and performance data with developers. We use this only to fix bugs and improve stability.

Children

Ten Plane is rated 4+ and contains no user-generated content, chat, or unrestricted web access. The app is not part of Apple’s “Made for Kids” program because it displays third-party advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data (high scores, settings) stays on your device until you delete the app. Uninstalling the app removes it. We do not hold a server-side copy of any user data because we do not operate any server that receives it.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is no user account or data set on our side to access, correct, or delete. For data handled by Google AdMob, please refer to Google’s controls and policies linked above.
